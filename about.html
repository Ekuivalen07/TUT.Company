Our Vision

A connected tech ecosystem where builders never stop growing.

We envision an industry where cutting-edge AI, software, web platforms, and robotics are not locked behind closed doors—but openly taught, practiced, and celebrated by learners from their very first line of code to senior engineering leadership.

Our Mission

  • Build Real Systems: Deliver reliable AI, software, web/app, and IoT robotics products for partners and communities.
  • Educate Without Barriers: Provide hybrid IT pathways via TUT.EDU (online) and TUT.Academy (offline) for beginners to pros.
  • Elevate Tech Culture: Stage national expos, hackathons, and robotics arenas through TUT.Entertainment.
